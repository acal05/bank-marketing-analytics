# Bank Marketing Business Questions

## Primary Business Problem

How can the bank improve the efficiency of its direct-marketing campaign by identifying customers who are most likely to subscribe to a term deposit?

## Customer Targeting

- Which customer characteristics are associated with higher subscription rates?
- Which customer segments should receive priority?
- Do age, occupation, education, marital status, or account balance influence subscription rates?

## Campaign Performance

- Which contact methods produce the highest conversion rates?
- Which months perform best?
- How does the number of campaign contacts affect conversion?
- Does prior campaign success predict future subscription?
- Do certain age groups have higher term deposit subscription rates?
- Do certain occupations have higher term deposit subscription rates?

## Statistical Analysis

- Which observed relationships are statistically significant?
- Which differences are meaningful enough to influence a business decision?

## Predictive Modeling

- Can a model identify customers who are more likely to subscribe?
- Which variables contribute most to predictions?
- What classification threshold best balances successful targeting and unnecessary calls?

## Business Impact

- How many customers could the bank avoid contacting while retaining most potential subscribers?
- How much better would model-based targeting perform than untargeted outreach?
- Which customer groups should receive increased or reduced marketing attention?
